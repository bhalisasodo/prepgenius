# **Prepgenious**

## 

### Phase 1



Below is a **Lean MVP Scope Definition** designed to validate your **AI interview-prep platform for contact centre roles** the **minimal possible complexity,** while still testing the core value hypothesis.



The guiding principle is: **prove that candidates perceive measurable improvement in interview readiness and are willing to pay for it.**



#### Lean MVP Scope Definition



**AI Interview Prep for Contact Centre Candidates**





##### 1\. **MVP Objective (Single Clear Hypothesis)**



> **Hypothesis:**

> Contact centre job candidates will pay for a domain-specific AI mock interview experience that closely mirrors real contact centre interviews and provides actionable feedback they cannot easily get elsewhere.



Everything in the MVP must directly serve validating this hypothesis.





##### 2\. **Target User (Extremely Narrow)**



###### Persona: **Entry-Level Inbound Contact Centre Candidate**



\* Applying for **customer support / call centre agent** roles

\* 0–2 years of experience

\* English-speaking

\* Interview format: phone or virtual

\* Geography: **single market only** (recommend: South Africa, India, or Philippines)



**Why this persona:**



\* High volume of candidates

\* Highly standardised interview structure

\* Strong anxiety around communication and scenarios

\* Price-sensitive but motivated



##### 3\. **Core User Journey (End-to-End)**



**Step 1:** Landing page → “Practice a real call centre interview”

**Step 2:** Short onboarding (role + experience level)

**Step 3:** AI mock interview (10–15 minutes, voice or text)

**Step 4:** Instant feedback report

**Step 5:** Paywall → unlock second interview + deeper feedback





##### 4\. **MVP Feature Set (Must-Have Only)**



###### 4.1 Interview Simulation (Core)

###### 

**Included**



\* 1 interview type only:



&nbsp; \* \*Inbound customer support agent\*

\* 8–12 common interview questions:



&nbsp; \* “Tell me about yourself”

&nbsp; \* “How do you handle an angry customer?”

&nbsp; \* “Describe a time you solved a problem”

\* AI acts as interviewer:



&nbsp; \* Structured

&nbsp; \* Neutral tone

&nbsp; \* Predictable flow



\*\*Excluded\*\*



\* Multiple roles

\* Personality variations

\* Live human interviews

\* Video interviews



---



\### 4.2 Input Modality (Choose One)



\*\*Recommendation:\*\*

👉 \*\*Text-based first\*\*, with optional voice added later.



\*\*Rationale\*\*



\* Faster to build

\* Lower compute costs

\* Easier evaluation logic

\* Avoids accent and ASR issues initially



---



##### 4.3 Feedback Engine (Critical Differentiator)



**Feedback Dimensions (Max 4)**



1\. \*\*Clarity \& Structure\*\*



&nbsp;  \* Did the candidate answer the question directly?

&nbsp;  \* STAR-style structure detection

2\. \*\*Customer Empathy Signals\*\*



&nbsp;  \* Use of customer-centric language

&nbsp;  \* Emotional acknowledgment

3\. \*\*Professional Communication\*\*



&nbsp;  \* Tone appropriateness

&nbsp;  \* Avoidance of slang or filler

4\. \*\*Role Fit\*\*



&nbsp;  \* Alignment with contact centre expectations



\*\*Feedback Output\*\*



\* Plain language explanations

\* “What you did well”

\* “What to improve”

\* One concrete improvement example



\*\*Explicitly Exclude\*\*



\* Numeric scores initially

\* Overly technical NLP explanations

\* AI confidence percentages



---



##### 4.4 Limited Practice Loop



\* Free tier:



&nbsp; \* 1 mock interview

&nbsp; \* High-level feedback

\* Paid tier:



&nbsp; \* 2–3 additional interviews

&nbsp; \* Side-by-side improvement hints



\*\*Goal\*\*

Test willingness to pay, not usage addiction.



---



##### 5\. **What the MVP Deliberately Does NOT Include**



This is as important as what you include.



\* CV parsing

\* Job description ingestion

\* Multilingual support

\* Gamification

\* Certification

\* Employer dashboards

\* Real-time coaching



Each of these is a **Phase 2+ feature.**





##### 6\. **Technical Architecture (Lean)**



\### Frontend



\* Web app (mobile responsive)

\* Single-page flow



\### Backend



\* LLM for:



&nbsp; \* Interview prompts

&nbsp; \* Response evaluation

\* Simple rules + LLM hybrid for feedback consistency



\### Data



\* Minimal storage:



&nbsp; \* Responses

&nbsp; \* Feedback summaries

\* No long-term user history initially



---



\## 7. \*\*Success Metrics (MVP Validation)\*\*



You are NOT measuring scale yet.



\### Primary Metrics



\* % of users who complete the full mock interview

\* % who say: \*“This felt like a real interview”\*

\* % willing to pay for a second session



\### Secondary Metrics



\* Self-reported confidence improvement

\* Drop-off point analysis



\*\*Red Flag Signals\*\*



\* Users say feedback is “generic”

\* Users complete interview but don’t read feedback

\* Zero conversion even at low price point



---



\## 8. \*\*Pricing Test (Early Signal Only)\*\*



\* Free: 1 interview

\* Paid: low-friction price point (e.g USD equivalent 5 - 10)



You are testing **perceived value\*\*, not revenue optimisation.**



##### 

##### 10\. **Decision Gate After MVP**



After 50–100 real users:



**Continue if:**



\* Users describe the experience as “specific” and “real”

\* At least some are willing to pay

\* Feedback meaningfully changes their answers in round two



\*\*Pivot if:\*\*



\* Value feels indistinguishable from generic AI tools

\* CAC exceeds realistic lifetime value

\* Users want features you intentionally excluded (strong signal of misalignment)



---



\## Final Strategic Note



This MVP is not a product.

It is an **experiment to validate a narrow promise**:



> \*“We help contact centre candidates practice exactly what they will be asked.”\*



If that promise resonates, scaling becomes an execution problem—not a market problem.

#### 2\. Feature Expansion Pillars (Only 4)

###### 

###### Each pillar addresses a distinct value lever.

###### 

##### Pillar 1: **Voice-Based Interview Simulation (Selective Rollout)**

##### 

##### Why Now

###### 

* ###### Contact centre interviews are **predominantly voice-based**
* ###### Voice unlocks differentiation that text-only competitors lack
* ###### You already validated interview flow and feedback logic in text

###### 

##### Scope (Lean)

###### 

* ###### One voice interview type only (Inbound Support)
* ###### Asynchronous voice:

######  	- User records answers

######  	- AI responds after each turn

* ###### No live interruption or real-time coaching yet

###### 

##### Voice Analytics (Very Focused)

###### 

* ###### Speech clarity
* ###### Pace (too fast / too slow)
* ###### Excessive filler detection

###### 

##### Explicit Exclusions



* ###### Accent scoring
* ###### Emotion classification beyond basic tone signals
* ###### Live voice agents

###### 

###### **Business impact:** Stronger realism → higher perceived value → higher pricing ceiling.

###### 

##### 

##### Pillar 2: **Structured Scoring \& Readiness Index**

##### 

##### Why

###### 

###### Users and employers need **clear signals**, not just advice.

###### 

##### Feature

###### 

###### Introduce a **Contact Centre Interview Readiness Index (CC-IRI)**:

###### 

* ###### Composite score across:

###### 

######   - Communication

######   - Empathy

######   - Structure

######   - Role alignment



* ###### Presented as:

###### 

######   - “Not Ready / Borderline / Interview Ready”

###### 

##### Critical Design Constraint

###### 

* ###### Scores must be:

###### 

######   - Directional, not absolute

######   - Framed as coaching signals, not pass/fail judgments

###### 

##### What This Enables Later

###### 

* ###### Progress tracking
* ###### Badging
* ###### Employer-facing signals

###### 

###### 

#### Pillar 3: **Role \& Scenario Depth (Not Breadth)**

###### 

##### Expand Vertically, Not Horizontally

###### 

###### Instead of adding many roles, deepen **one or two**.

###### 

###### **Example Expansion for Inbound Support**

###### 

* ###### Angry customer scenario
* ###### Policy limitation scenario
* ###### Escalation handoff scenario

###### 

###### Each scenario:

###### 

* ###### Introduces situational pressure
* ###### Tests empathy and judgment
* ###### Reuses the same evaluation framework

###### 

##### Why This Matters

###### 

###### Contact centre interviews are scenario-heavy. This increases **domain credibility** without increasing surface area too much.

###### 

#### Pillar 4: **Outcome-Oriented Feedback Loop**

###### 

##### From Advice → Action

###### 

###### Add:

###### 

* ###### “Improved answer example”
* ###### “Rewrite your response” mode
* ###### Side-by-side comparison (original vs improved)

###### 

##### Optional (Very Powerful)

###### 

* ###### AI asks: **“Would you like to try this question again using the feedback?”**

###### 

###### This directly links feedback to observable improvement.

# Phase 3: B2B \& Ecosystem Integration

###### 

###### **From Candidate Prep → Hiring Infrastructure**

###### 

###### 

#### Phase 3 Core Objective

###### 

###### > **Convert candidate-side readiness signals into employer-side hiring efficiency.**

###### 

###### In practical terms:

###### 

* ###### Employers hire faster
* ###### Recruiters screen better
* ###### Candidates perform more consistently

###### 

###### Phase 3 is not about “selling software to enterprises” immediately.

###### It is about **embedding yourself into hiring workflows** in a way that is difficult to replace.

###### 

###### 

#### 1\. Target B2B Customer Segments (Ordered by Ease of Entry)

###### 

##### Tier 1: **Recruitment Agencies (BPO Specialists)**

###### 

* ###### High hiring volumes
* ###### Immediate pain: screening costs and candidate quality variance
* ###### Low procurement friction compared to enterprises

###### 

##### Tier 2: **Training \& Skills Development Providers**

###### 

* ###### Incentivised to show placement success
* ###### Already preparing candidates, but lack objective readiness signals

###### 

##### Tier 3: **Mid-Size Contact Centres (100–1,000 seats)**

###### 

* ###### Direct ROI case (reduced time-to-hire, lower early attrition)
* ###### Will pilot tools faster than large enterprises

###### 

###### **Important:**

###### Large multinational BPOs come last. They require proof, references, and data.

###### 

###### ---

###### 

#### 2\. Phase 3 Value Propositions (Employer-Side)

##### 

##### A. Candidate Readiness Signal (Primary)

###### 

###### Introduce a **standardised, explainable readiness summary**, not raw AI outputs.

###### 

###### **Example:**

###### 

* ###### Overall readiness level
* ###### Key strengths
* ###### Risk flags (e.g. empathy under pressure)
* ###### Recommended interview focus areas

###### 

###### This reframes your platform as **decision support**, not decision automation.

###### 

###### 

##### B. Screening Time Compression

###### 

###### Replace:

###### 

* ###### Long first-round interviews

######    With:

* ###### Short validation interviews informed by readiness data

###### 

###### **Outcome:**

###### Recruiters focus time where it matters.

###### 

###### 

##### C. Quality \& Consistency Improvement

###### 

* ###### Candidates arrive better prepared
* ###### Interviewers see more structured answers
* ###### Less variance between interviewers

###### 

###### This is particularly valuable in high-volume BPO hiring.

###### 

######

##### 3.1 Employer / Recruiter Dashboard (Minimal)

###### 

###### **Includes:**

######

* ###### Filter by readiness tier

######

###### 

* ###### Deep analytics
* ###### Custom dashboards
* ###### Real-time monitoring

######

##### 3.2 Shareable Readiness Reports

##### 

###### Candidates can:

######

######

######

###### 

###### Do NOT allow full custom scoring yet.
